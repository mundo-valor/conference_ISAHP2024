🏛️ About ISAHP & Its Significance

The International Symposium on the Analytic Hierarchy Process (ISAHP) for Decision Making brings together researchers, academics, students, and other users of AHP/ANP from around the globe to share their advanced research, experiences, and breakthroughs in decision-making methodologies.

ISAHP is a biennial conference dedicated entirely to multi-criteria decision analysis, placing a particular focus on the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) and its powerful extension, the Analytic Network Process (ANP)—both originally developed by Thomas L. Saaty—as well as their combinations and comparisons with other quantitative methods. Presenting a paper at ISAHP represents a major milestone in validating mathematical and decision-analytic models against rigorous international peer review.
